# XVI Research Park design brief

- Player promise: Walk through Flood Sung's research park, discover the work through short interactions, and leave with a clear map of the research arc.
- Feeling: curious, tactile, calm, and rewarding; a toy-like knowledge city with readable silhouettes.
- Primary verb: explore and scan.
- Secondary verbs: move, jump, dash, choose, unlock, read.
- 5–30 second repeat: navigate toward a landmark, read its signal, answer one content-grounded question.
- 1–5 minute change: three paper landmarks become Paper Cards and unlock the Concept Board.
- Loss/retry: incorrect challenge answer leaves the player in the same place and can be retried immediately.
- Reward: verified title/year/citation/source card plus persistent archive progress.
- Non-goals: combat, fabricated paper claims, an empty infinite universe.
