# Core loop contract

Player explores toward a landmark to reveal a verified research signal while route choice and short questions create attention pressure; success grants a Paper Card and unlock progress on the Concept Board, while a wrong answer costs only a retry and teaches the underlying idea.

Inputs: WASD/arrow movement, Space jump, Shift dash, E scan, pointer drag camera, touch map buttons. State: roam -> nearLandmark -> challenge -> reward/archive. Persistence: localStorage key `xviResearchProgress`.
