# Level plan

A compact 12-sided floating diorama is viewed from a 3/4 third-person camera. The player starts in the central plaza at the research train, with three landmarks readable ahead: Compare Lab (left), Proof Library (right), Agent Foundry (back). The ring road gives a safe route and the train/map buttons provide recovery navigation.

The first decision is which beacon to approach. Each landmark exposes one 15–30 second question. Completing Compare, Proof, and Agent unlocks the Concept Board (3/3). Archive, Career, and Contact remain accessible from fast-travel HUD panels. There are no lethal hazards; wrong answers retry instantly so the site stays welcoming.
